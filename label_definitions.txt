analysis: makes an argument backed by a specific, checkable fact
hot_take: a confident claim with no support offered
reaction: an immediate response to something that just happened

opinion: Makes a claim and supports it with at least one reason.
personal_anecdote: A comentator describes something they noticed from their personal life that was impacted by the topic being discussed. They then tend to add to that summary their own viewpoint that is heavily linked to how they were impacted by the changes.
assertion: A commentator asserts a belief or a viewpoint without support to back up the claim. Usually it will lack the evidence.
